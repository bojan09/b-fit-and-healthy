The back is not one muscle. Several layers coordinate the shoulder blades, upper arms, spine, and pelvis. Understanding their jobs makes exercise selection clearer.

## Latissimus dorsi

The lats bring the upper arm down and back. Pulldowns and rows train them, but the shoulder blade should still move naturally rather than being forced down throughout every repetition.

## Trapezius and shoulder-blade control

Different trapezius fibers elevate, retract, and upwardly rotate the shoulder blade. Rows, carries, and overhead work challenge different parts of this system.

## Spinal erectors

These long muscles help extend and stabilize the spine. Hip hinges and loaded carries train them to resist unwanted movement, not simply to create a large back arch.

## Train movements, then refine

Begin with a horizontal pull, a vertical pull, and a hinge you can control. Add specialized work only when a clear goal or limitation justifies it.
